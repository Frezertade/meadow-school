# Meadow Scope & Sequence

> Academic backbone. Quarters are flexible pacing guides, not deadlines —
> especially for ages 3–4, where play leads and repetition is the curriculum.
> Each outcome has a stable `skill` slug. Exercises carry `skillId` so the
> mastery engine (Phase 3) and parent skills grid can track real progress.

Conventions: ✅ covered by a shipped lesson · ⬜ gap (see backlog) ·
`skill-slug` = exercise-level skill id.
